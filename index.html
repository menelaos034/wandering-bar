Andrew Papaloucas

Founder & Professional Bartender

10Years of hospitality & bartending experience

With 10 years of experience across London and Cyprus, I have worked in luxury hotels, high-volume bars, restaurants and private members' clubs, developing extensive experience in professional bartending and event service.

Throughout my career, I have progressed to Head Bartender and F&B Supervisor/Bar Manager, developing extensive experience in cocktail preparation, premium bar service, beverage operations, stock control and team leadership.
